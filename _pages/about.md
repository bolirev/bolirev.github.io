I am a Data Engineer at [eWolff in the Dr. Wolff Group](https://www.drwolffgroup.com/) in Bielefeld. There I work with teams across the company to acquire, model, and prepare data for analyses — building ELT pipelines, shaping warehouse models, and putting data quality and contracts where decisions actually need them. On a given day I may be structuring data with stakeholders, developing extraction and load paths, coordinating delivery, or exploring relationships in the data itself.

Before that I spent years as a researcher and Akademischer Rat at Bielefeld University (with a PhD in neurobiology and behavior), studying insect navigation, collision avoidance, and how agents find their way in cluttered environments. That habit of asking how systems decide — and what information they need — still shapes how I approach data work.

On the side I write and experiment: LLM debates and multi-agent setups, tool use for hard puzzles, and small data products around finance and planning. Code tends to land on [GitHub](https://github.com/bolirev); longer write-ups also appear on [Medium](https://medium.com/@olivier.bolirev).

Beside the keyboard, I am a fervent bike rider and like training for dancing shows.
