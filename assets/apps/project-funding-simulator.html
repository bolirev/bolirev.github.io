Each project is a bill on a date. A hard date uses the latest obligation in the list that matures on or before that month, and that bond is held to its own date. That locks the yield only if it was bought at or below par. The months after it matures, and any bill with no such bond, earn Tagesgeld. Interest on both is taxed once a year, and any interest not yet taxed is taxed when the money is spent. Projects that can wait, and any saving the dated bills do not need, go to an accumulating ETF, which is taxed on the gain when the shares are sold.

Paste a CSV, or the file format

Projects: name,cost_today,due,flexible. Dates are YYYY-MM. flexible is yes or no (blank means the date cannot move). Obligations: name,yield,due. yield is the pre-tax percent if the bond is bought at or below par. Inflation and tax are the controls above. Returns: a date column, then one column of monthly simple returns per history. 0.012 or 1.2% means +1.2% that month. One row per month, oldest first, no gaps. A column of index levels named level is converted. Drop the file on this page, or save it as assets/data/equity-returns.csv.
