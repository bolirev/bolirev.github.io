Each project is a bill on a date. A hard date is matched to an obligation that matures that month and is held until then, which locks the yield. A bill due inside a year stays in Tagesgeld. Projects that can wait, and any saving the dated bills do not need, go to the ETF.

Projects: name,cost_today,due,flexible. Dates are YYYY-MM. flexible is yes or no (blank means the date cannot move). Inflation is the slider. Returns: a date column, then one column of monthly simple returns per history. 0.012 or 1.2% means +1.2% that month. One row per month, oldest first, no gaps. A column of index levels named level is converted. Drop the file on this page, or save it as assets/data/equity-returns.csv.
